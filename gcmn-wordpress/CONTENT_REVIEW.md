## The full copy of the old website

The site now carries everything saved in the offline copy of gcmnigeria.org (`C:\My Web Sites\Indigitous`): 448 pages were read, and every real page, article and testimony is on the new site at its old address, with the old address redirecting.

What is **not** there, and why (please check against the live old site):

- **96 addresses were error pages in the copy** (the old site showed "404" for them when the copy was made). 28 of them are the per-language pages under Language Resources (Albanian, Arabic, ... Vietnamese); the Language Resources page is there, its language links are not. The others include the Mission Trips page, the LeaderImpact sub-pages, the Communities sub-pages and the Social Media training pages that exist only under Digital Ministry.
- **Background pictures** come from the old site's own library (the copy has about 340 large pictures). A page uses the picture for its topic when the old site had one, otherwise its section's picture. Check the match on pages that matter to you; any picture can be changed in Elementor.
- **Most photographs.** The copy was made while the old site blocked picture downloads (403), so only 12 pictures were saved. Pages show text where no picture exists. Add pictures in Elementor.
- **Many articles are the first part only.** The old site held an excerpt and linked to the full article on another Cru site; the copy saved the excerpt.
- **Pages that duplicated another page** (the same text under two addresses, such as Social Media under Training and under Digital Ministry) appear once, and the second address redirects.
- **US-only pages of other Cru countries** (Argentina, Brazil, and so on) are not part of the Nigerian site and were not loaded.
- **Legal pages** (Terms of Use, Privacy) are the old site's own text and mention Cru entities. Have them reviewed before launch.
- **External links** (for example to cru.org or everystudent.com) are kept as they were and open the other site.
